What it does

  • Conflict-free booking. The system checks every doctor's schedule before confirming a slot, so two patients can never be booked into the same doctor at the same time.
  • Fair cancellations. Cancel in good time and it's free. Cancel late (inside the 24-hour window) and a small fee is applied automatically — no arguing at the counter.
  • A doctor's whole day, at a glance. Pick a doctor and a date to see every booked slot in order.
  • Find a patient fast. Search appointments by patient name instead of scrolling a paper diary.

Who it's for

Small and mid-sized clinics — a handful of doctors, one or two front-desk staff, and a steady stream of walk-ins and phone bookings — where a shared paper diary or spreadsheet has started causing real double-bookings.

What we'd build next

  1. SMS/WhatsApp reminders the day before an appointment, to cut down on late cancellations and no-shows.
  2. Per-doctor working hours and holiday calendars, so the system also blocks bookings outside a doctor's actual availability.
  3. A basic analytics view for the clinic manager: no-show rate, late-cancellation revenue, busiest doctor/day.
